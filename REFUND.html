UUGet — Refund Policy

Last updated: 2026-11-18

This policy describes how refunds work for UUGet credit packs and Pro subscriptions. Payments are processed by Creem as merchant of record; Creem's payment terms also apply.

Failed downloads are never charged

Credits and free-quota usage are reserved when a download starts and are only committed when bytes have been transferred. If a download fails or is cancelled before any bytes are transferred, the reservation is released and nothing is consumed. If some bytes were transferred before a failure, the download is treated as used.

Credit packs

Credits are prepaid and consumed per download. You may request a full refund within 14 days of purchase if none of the credits from that purchase have been used. Once any credit from a pack has been consumed, the pack is treated as used and is no longer refundable except where required by law.

If you believe a charge is incorrect — for example, a duplicate purchase or a charge for a download that never started — contact us and we will investigate and restore credits or issue a refund where appropriate.

Pro subscriptions

You can cancel a Pro subscription at any time through the customer portal link in the extension. Cancellation stops future renewals; access continues until the end of the current paid period. Partial periods are not refunded except where required by law.

Statutory rights

If you are a consumer in a jurisdiction with mandatory withdrawal or refund rights (for example, the EU/EEA or UK), those rights apply in addition to this policy. Where a statutory withdrawal right applies and you have already consumed the purchased credits or used the subscription, we may deduct the value of the used portion as permitted by law.

Chargebacks and reversals

If a payment is reversed through a chargeback or refund, the credits or subscription it granted are removed. If those credits were already spent, your balance may become negative and will be offset by future credits or purchases.

How to request a refund

Email support@uu0uu.com from the email address used at checkout, and include:

  • The Creem order or subscription identifier, if available
  • The approximate purchase date and product
  • A short description of the issue

We aim to respond within 5 business days. Approved refunds are issued to the original payment method through Creem; processing time depends on your bank or payment provider.
