UUGet — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-11-18

UUGet is a Chrome extension that detects and downloads video and audio streams from web pages. This policy describes what data UUGet handles and what it does not do.

Data UUGet does not collect

UUGet does not collect, transmit, sell, or share any personal information beyond what is described under Billing and licensing. Specifically, UUGet does not collect:

  • Personally identifiable information beyond the purchase email described below (name, address, phone number, government identifiers)
  • Health information
  • Payment card or bank details — payments are processed by Creem; UUGet never sees or stores card numbers
  • Authentication credentials for other services
  • Personal communications
  • Location
  • Browsing or web history
  • Clicks, mouse movements, or other user activity
  • Page content beyond the metadata required to name a download (title, thumbnail, canonical URL)

Data UUGet stores locally on your device

UUGet uses chrome.storage.local and chrome.storage.session to persist the following on your machine only. This data never leaves your browser:

  • User preferences (theme, filename template, history retention limit, merge toggles, browser-download-interceptor toggle, connections-per-file, auto-close-on-complete, one-time hint dismissal flags)
  • Download history (title, source URL, file size, timestamp) — retention is user-configurable and can be cleared at any time from the extension options page
  • In-progress download state (stage, bytes downloaded, per-chunk completion status for HTTP Range downloads) — used to render live progress and to enable byte-level resume of paused downloads within the same browser session. Cleared when the download finishes, is cancelled, or the offscreen document restarts
  • Billing state (see Billing and licensing) — anonymous device ID, device bearer token, cached entitlement snapshot, and a queue of download reports waiting to be sent

During active downloads, UUGet writes segment data to the Origin Private File System (OPFS) as scratch space for local WebAssembly remuxing and resume. These scratch files are deleted as soon as the download completes or is cancelled, and any orphaned files left behind by a crash are purged the next time the extension's offscreen document starts.

Browser download interceptor (opt-in)

Starting in v1.1.0, UUGet can offer to handle browser-initiated video and audio downloads through its own pipeline instead of the browser's native flow. The setting is on by default and can be disabled from the extension's Options page.

When the interceptor is on, UUGet:

  • Listens to chrome.downloads.onCreated — a browser event fired locally on your machine when any download starts
  • Reads the destination URL, MIME type, file size, suggested filename, and referring page from the event payload, all of which the browser would already pass to the Chrome download UI
  • Filters out non-media downloads (everything that is not video/*, audio/*, or a large opaque media-extension download)
  • Cancels the native download for matching cases and shows an in-page dialog asking whether to route the file through UUGet or proceed with the browser's native handling

None of this information is transmitted anywhere. The event payload is read in the extension's local service worker, used solely to render the dialog, and discarded once the user makes a choice. Downloads from blob:, data:, and chrome-extension: URLs (i.e. UUGet's own outputs) are ignored to avoid intercepting our own files. The interceptor never operates on YouTube, YouTube-nocookie, or googlevideo hosts.

Remote servers

UUGet does not use analytics, telemetry, crash reporting, or third-party tracking. Network requests fall into two groups:

  • Media fetches for the video and audio streams the user explicitly chose to download, sent directly to the origin servers hosting that media, plus page metadata (title, og:image) on the page the user is currently viewing
  • Billing requests to the UUGet billing service (a Cloudflare Worker) described under Billing and licensing — only when billing features are in use

Billing and licensing

UUGet offers a free daily download quota and optional paid credit packs and Pro subscriptions. To enforce quotas, apply credits and subscriptions, restore purchases, and prevent abuse, the extension talks to the UUGet billing service when billing is enabled. The data involved is limited to:

  • Anonymous device ID — a random identifier generated by the extension; it is not derived from hardware, account or browsing data
  • Device bearer token — authenticates the extension to the billing service. The service stores only a hash of this token, never the token itself
  • Purchase email — the email address used at Creem checkout, so purchases and subscriptions can be linked to your account and restored on another device with your License Key
  • Purchase and subscription state — product, plan, status, current period end, credit balance and ledger entries
  • Usage counters — the number of authorized downloads per UTC day, the download key, outcome and bytes downloaded, used to reserve quota and settle charges
  • License activations — which devices have activated a Creem License Key (up to 3)

Payment card details are entered on Creem's checkout page and processed by Creem as merchant of record; UUGet never receives or stores card numbers. Creem's own privacy policy applies to payment processing.

The billing service runs on Cloudflare Workers with a Cloudflare D1 database. Cloudflare may retain standard request logs (such as IP address and timestamp) under its own policies. Data is retained while the account is active; download authorization and ledger records are kept for accounting and fraud prevention.

Offline grace. When the billing service cannot be reached, the extension uses a locally cached entitlement snapshot signed by the billing service. The snapshot is valid for up to 72 hours and contains only the anonymous device ID, plan and subscription state, credit balance, free-quota usage, and issue/expiry timestamps. Download reports generated while offline are queued locally and sent when the connection returns.

Account deletion. You can delete your billing account and associated data by sending a request from the extension to DELETE /v1/account (device-authenticated) or by emailing support@uu0uu.com from your purchase email. Deletion revokes device tokens, license activations and subscriptions, and anonymizes the account's email and Creem customer identifier. Records required for accounting, tax and fraud prevention (purchases and ledger entries) are retained.

Permissions

UUGet's Chrome permissions are documented in the Chrome Web Store listing. Each is scoped to the extension's single purpose: detecting media on the current page and saving downloads to the user's Downloads folder. The downloads permission is additionally used by the optional browser-download interceptor described above. Host access to the billing service is used only for the billing requests described above.

Children

UUGet is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect data from anyone. Paid features are intended for adults or with guardian consent.

Changes

If this policy changes, the "Last updated" date at the top will change. Material changes will be noted in the extension's release notes.
